RISK FACTORS
================================================================================

1. CHINA EXPORT RESTRICTIONS (HIGH IMPACT)
   New U.S. government export controls significantly restrict our ability to 
   sell advanced AI chips to China. We estimate this may impact approximately 
   $5 billion in annual revenue. We are developing different chips for the China 
   market, but there is no guarantee these will be approved or commercially viable.

2. SUPPLY CHAIN CONSTRAINTS (MEDIUM IMPACT)
   Demand for our H100 and upcoming H200 GPUs continues to significantly exceed 
   supply. Current lead times remain extended at 6-9 months. We are working with 
   TSMC to increase production capacity, but supply constraints may persist 
   through fiscal 2025.

3. CUSTOMER CONCENTRATION (MEDIUM IMPACT)
   Our top four customers (major cloud providers) represent approximately 46% of 
   total revenue. The loss of, or significant reduction in orders from, any of 
   these customers could materially impact our business.

4. COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE (EMERGING RISK)
   AMD's MI300 accelerator and Intel's Gaudi processors are gaining traction in 
   the market. While we maintain technology leadership, increased competition may 
   pressure margins in the future. We expect market share pressure to increase 
   in calendar year 2025.

5. GEOPOLITICAL TENSIONS
   Escalating tensions between the U.S. and China, as well as potential conflicts 
   in Taiwan (where our primary manufacturing partner TSMC is located), present 
   ongoing risks to our supply chain and market access.
