ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS
================================================================================

RISKS RELATING TO BUSINESS AND INDUSTRY

1. SUPPLY CHAIN AND MANUFACTURING RISKS

The Company's products are manufactured by outsourcing partners, primarily 
located in Asia. A significant concentration of manufacturing occurs in China. 
The Company has various forms of arrangements with suppliers, which may 
include providing financial support and making equipment available. Disruptions 
to manufacturing operations due to natural disasters, public health issues, 
geopolitical tensions, or other factors could materially impact product 
availability and financial results.

Specific concerns include:
- Over 90% of iPhone production occurs in China
- Taiwan Semiconductor (TSMC) is sole supplier of advanced chips
- Geopolitical tensions between U.S. and China present ongoing risks
- COVID-19 demonstrated vulnerability of concentrated manufacturing

2. PRODUCT TRANSITION AND DEMAND RISKS

The Company has historically experienced different levels of demand for its 
products during transition periods. If new products are not accepted by 
customers, or if the Company fails to accurately forecast demand, it may 
experience excess inventory or supply shortages.

Key product cycle risks:
- iPhone replacement cycles lengthening (now 4+ years average)
- Services growth dependent on installed base expansion
- Vision Pro adoption slower than internal projections
- Increasing smartphone competition in emerging markets

3. COMPETITIVE ENVIRONMENT

The markets for the Company's products and services are highly competitive. 
The Company faces substantial competition in all areas of its business, 
including from companies that have greater technical, marketing, financial, 
or other resources.

Competitive threats include:
- Samsung and Chinese OEMs in smartphones
- Google in services and operating systems
- Microsoft in enterprise and productivity
- Meta and emerging players in AR/VR

4. REGULATORY AND LEGAL RISKS

The Company is subject to various legal proceedings, claims, investigations, 
and regulatory oversight worldwide. Changes in laws and regulations could 
materially impact operations.

Current regulatory concerns:
- EU Digital Markets Act compliance costs
- App Store practices under antitrust scrutiny (Epic Games, DOJ)
- Privacy regulations affecting advertising revenue
- Right-to-repair legislation impacting service margins

5. FOREIGN EXCHANGE AND MACROECONOMIC RISKS

The Company's operations and performance depend significantly on worldwide 
economic conditions. A strong U.S. dollar negatively impacts international 
revenue when translated. Economic downturns could reduce consumer spending 
on premium devices.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
================================================================================

FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE SUMMARY

Total Net Revenue: $383.3 billion
- Products: $298.1 billion (down 4% YoY)
- Services: $85.2 billion (up 14% YoY)

Gross Margin: 44.1% (up from 43.3%)
- Products: 36.5%
- Services: 70.8%

Net Income: $97.0 billion
EPS: $6.13 (diluted)

Cash and Marketable Securities: $162.1 billion
Long-term Debt: $95.3 billion

SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

Americas: $162.6 billion (42% of revenue)
Europe: $94.3 billion (25% of revenue)
Greater China: $72.6 billion (19% of revenue)
Japan: $24.9 billion (7% of revenue)
Rest of Asia Pacific: $28.9 billion (7% of revenue)

Note: Greater China revenue declined 8% year-over-year due to increased 
competition from Huawei and other domestic brands following easing of 
U.S. chip restrictions.

SERVICES BUSINESS

Services revenue reached record levels, driven by:
- App Store: Strong developer ecosystem (>1.8 million apps)
- Apple Music: 100+ million subscribers
- iCloud: Storage upgrades driving revenue
- AppleCare: High attach rates on premium devices
- Advertising: Search Ads growth despite privacy changes

Services gross margin of 70.8% significantly exceeds products margin, 
supporting overall profitability as product mix shifts.

================================================================================
